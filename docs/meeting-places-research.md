# Coffee meeting places in San Francisco

Checked 19 October 2026. Added ten places to the existing 19, covering downtown, the Mission, Mission Bay, Dogpatch, the Marina, and the Outer Richmond.

The selection favours a place to sit and talk, good coffee, usable meeting hours, and a clear public entrance. Meeting fit is a judgment from published venue descriptions and seating reports; a high coffee rating alone does not establish it. Noise and seat availability depend on the visit. Walking convenience still determines ranking within the curated shortlist.

## Hours conflicts resolved

**Blue Bottle South Park:** the official HTML renders every day as closed, but its public `__NEXT_DATA__` contains `props.pageProps.cafe.hours` with seven entries, each open `06:30` and close `17:00`. The record identifies South Park at 2 South Park and was updated on 10 June 2026. Those official published hours are used. Restaurantji and Roadtrippers instead list 5:30am–6pm weekdays and 6:30am–6pm weekends; the app therefore flags the discrepancy. The official café coordinates are used, corroborated by the independent map listing.

**Golden Goat:** the visit page's main text says weekdays 8am–3pm and Saturday 9am–2pm, while its footer and FAQ footer say Monday–Thursday 8am–3pm, Friday 8am–noon, and Saturday temporarily closed. The app uses the shared weekday window, ends Friday at noon, and excludes Saturday. Sunday has no confirmed opening schedule. The shortlist asks users to confirm Friday/Saturday hours. This is a conservative choice among conflicting official statements, not a claim that the conflict has been resolved by the café.

**Delah SoMa:** its location directory says 11pm closing every day, while its contact page lists 10pm on Monday–Thursday and Sunday. The app uses the earlier per-day closing times and flags late-evening hours for confirmation. Friday and Saturday end at 11pm in both sources.

**La Promenade:** its official site confirms the café but publishes no usable schedule. Cylex and Zmenu agree on 7:30am–5pm weekdays and 8am–5pm weekends. These are business-listing hours, labelled accordingly; they are not operator-confirmed hours.

## Data and implementation

All ten additions have unique, stable IDs and remain in alphabetical order. Nine use exact ArcGIS PointAddress matches with score 100. South Park uses Blue Bottle's own coordinates, within roughly 10 metres of its ArcGIS address match. Fort Mason's street-address point was also cross-checked against an ArcGIS café POI within roughly 25 metres. Andytown uses the street entrance at 181 Fremont; its seventh-floor access is stated in the address and shortlist note.

The source pages, extracted Blue Bottle record, and address-match responses were retained under `/tmp/coffee-meetup-research/`. The existing 30-minute opening-window filter, warning below one hour, calendar duration, and walking-balance ranking apply to all additions. This pass adds researched venues and source notes; it does not introduce review-score ranking or assume that a free table is guaranteed.
